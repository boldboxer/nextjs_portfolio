This is one of my largest and most impactful projects: a **full School Information Management System** that centralizes academic and financial operations.

## Features
- **ERP-style dashboards** with analytics and reporting.
- Finance & accounting module (initial focus).
- Student grades, assignments, and performance tracking.
- Attendance and administration tools.
- Role-based authentication (staff, admin, students).

## Ongoing Improvements
- Currently working on **bank API** and **M-Pesa integrations** (since Aug 2025) for accurate and automated financial reporting.
- Rolling updates to academic features based on school feedback.

## Technologies
- Next.js (frontend)  
- Node.js + Express (backend APIs)  
- MySQL (database)  
- TailwindCSS  
- Private cloud hosting
