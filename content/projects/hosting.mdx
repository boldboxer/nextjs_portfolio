Beyond building applications, I also handle **hosting and infrastructure**. This involves both **managed platforms (like Vercel)** and **self-managed private cloud servers**.

## Hosting Experience
- Hosted school websites and travel agency sites on **Vercel**.
- Deployed SIMS applications on a **private cloud server** for greater control.
- Configured domains, SSL certificates, and continuous deployment.

## Recommendations
While **Vercel** is great for speed and ease, some projects (like SIMS) require **self-hosting** for compliance and data privacy.
