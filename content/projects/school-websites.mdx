## Features
- Event calendars and announcements section.
- Admissions and enrollment forms.
- Staff and departmental pages.
- SEO-friendly structure to increase online visibility.

## Technologies
- Next.js
- TailwindCSS
- Vercel (deployment)

## Hosting
I also manage hosting and updates, ensuring that the sites remain secure and available.

These projects demonstrate my ability to work on **end-to-end web presence for institutions**, from design to deployment.
