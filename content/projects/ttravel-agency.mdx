A project for a local travel agency to highlight their services and improve customer bookings.

## Features
- Display of travel packages with pricing.
- Contact and inquiry forms.
- Mobile-first design for travelers browsing on the go.
- SEO optimization for search engines.

## Technologies
- Next.js
- TailwindCSS
- Vercel

## Hosting
Deployed and hosted on **Vercel**, with regular updates.

## Recommendation
This project taught me the importance of **visual storytelling** in web design, especially for industries like travel.
