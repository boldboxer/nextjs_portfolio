My latest project is a **WordPress-based e-commerce store**, built collaboratively with another developer. Unlike my other full-stack projects, this one was focused more on **consultation and optimization**.

## Contributions
- Guided use of **SEO-friendly content** and product descriptions.
- Helped refine **store design and UX**.
- Integrated payment methods suited for the Kenyan market (since Stripe is not available here).
- Provided insights into **performance optimization**.

## Technologies
- WordPress
- WooCommerce
- SEO tools
- Shared hosting

## Conclusion
This project highlights my ability to adapt to **different ecosystems** (not only Next.js) and ensure solutions are **localized** for the Kenyan market.
